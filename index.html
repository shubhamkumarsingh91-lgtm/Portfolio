Compliance AI Agent: from policy change to updated procedure

Python, rule engine, Qwen2.5-7B, FAISS, MCP server. In development.

Problem
When a Medicaid policy changes, someone has to find what changed, work out which internal procedures it affects, and rewrite them by hand. Misses surface later as audit findings.
What I built
An agent that compares policy versions, traces each change to the affected procedures, and drafts the edits as reviewable diffs. Nothing changes without a named approver, and every action is logged. It also runs as an MCP server for AI assistants.
Results
The first version, built on a language model, passed 6 of 10 test cases. A rule-based engine now passes all 10 development cases and all 8 blind cases, and 96.7% to 98.5% of checks across 200 generated policy manuals. Tested on one real CMS document pair (MITA 2.0 to 3.0).

AI Paper Trading Lab

Python, XGBoost, LightGBM, Alpaca API, GitHub Actions

Problem
Can a self-learning model beat simply holding the S&P 500, once it is measured honestly?
What I built
Three scheduled bots on simulated-money accounts: two intraday bots driven by a 54-feature model that retrains nightly on its own trade outcomes, behind fixed risk rules, and a daily strategy that switches between SPY and a sideways-market playbook.
Results
No live track record yet. The model shows only a small edge over chance, and the 200-day backtest showed none. The regime strategy beat SPY in a backtest (15.9% against 13.7% a year), which is likely an upper bound.
