Compliance AI Agent

Python, rule engine, Qwen2.5-7B, FAISS, MCP server

The problem
When a Medicaid policy changes, someone must find what changed, work out which procedures it affects, and rewrite them by hand. Misses surface later as audit findings.
What it does
Compares policy versions, traces each change to the affected procedures, and drafts the edits as diffs with review flags. Nothing changes without a named approver, and every action is logged.
How well it works
The language-model version passed 6 of 10 test cases. The rule engine that replaced it passes 10 of 10 development and 8 of 8 blind cases, and 96.7% to 98.5% of checks on 200 generated policy manuals.
